About the Studio

Hi, I'm Arrian

A licensed esthetician passionate about helping clients achieve healthy, radiant skin through customized, results-driven treatments in a relaxing environment. My goal is to provide every client with personalized care, education, and a spa experience that leaves them feeling refreshed and confident.

At your first visit, we'll begin with a detailed skin consultation to discuss your skin concerns, goals, lifestyle, and current skincare routine. I'll evaluate your skin and create a treatment plan tailored specifically to your needs. Whether you're looking to improve acne, brighten your complexion, reduce signs of aging, or simply relax, every facial is customized just for you.

My Approach to Skincare

I believe that every person's skin is unique, which is why I customize every treatment based on your skin type and concerns. My focus is on improving your skin's health while delivering a relaxing and rejuvenating experience.

I use professional DBH (Dermaesthetics Beverly Hills) skincare products to cleanse, exfoliate, hydrate, and nourish the skin. For advanced treatments, I incorporate Rejuran during nano needling to help improve skin texture, hydration, elasticity, and overall skin rejuvenation.

My Services May Include

  • Customized facials using professional DBH skincare products
  • Nano Needling with Rejuran to enhance product absorption and promote smoother, healthier-looking skin
  • Microdermabrasion to gently exfoliate dead skin cells, improve skin texture, and reveal a brighter complexion
  • Gentle extractions (when appropriate)
  • Hydrating and calming masks
  • Targeted serums and moisturizers selected for your skin's specific needs

My Treatments Are Ideal For Clients Looking To Improve

  • Fine lines and early signs of aging
  • Dull or dehydrated skin
  • Uneven skin tone and texture
  • Hyperpigmentation and sun damage
  • Mild acne and post-acne marks
  • Overall skin health and radiance

My goal is to help you achieve beautiful, healthy skin through consistent care, professional products, and personalized treatment plans while providing a relaxing spa experience you'll look forward to every visit.
